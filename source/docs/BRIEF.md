# Kingdom Crowns — first design pass (Nov 9, 2026)

This is a real slot we will build after design, not only a portfolio case. Structure matches Arman Antonian’s Hungry Wolf case (https://armanantonian.com/public-works/hungry-wolf) but the art, characters and world must be original. Do not copy Hungry Wolf’s farmer, wolf, farm, symbols or UI chrome. Local structure reference only: /workspace/slots/kingdom-crowns/ref/

## This pass (stop after this; Final UI is a later pass)
1. Logo and visual identity for **Kingdom Crowns**, plus a 3D crown.
2. Top banner like Hungry Wolf’s: logo centered, background a kingdom with swords and other medieval attributes.
3. Intro video: the king comes to the palace, sits on his throne, the crown drops onto his head, then the slot starts.
4. Wireframes of the game, including the symbol set and paytable.
5. Exactly one concept board: gaming symbols plus one king sitting on the left, showing all visual elements.

## Slot layout
- Main grid: 5 columns × 3 rows.
- Extra top row shaped like a crown: cell, empty, cell, empty, cell (symbols only in columns 1, 3 and 5).
- The whole slot should read as a crown.
- Top-row cells may contain only wild, scatter, or empty.

## Symbols
wild, scatter, crown, sword, shield, 7, A, K, Q, J, 10

## Line pays (as given by Davit; do not invent extras)
- Crown line: 1000x (1/1000 chance to drop a line)
- 7: 500x
- Sword: 100x
- Shield: 75x
- A: 40x
- K: 30x
- Q: 25x
- J: 20x
- 10: 10x
Wild and scatter are specials; no extra pays were given.
