# Kingdom Crowns — Direction (first pass, Nov 9 2026)

## World
Dusk stone kingdom and torch-lit grey-stone throne hall. Royal blue banners with a gold crown emblem, swords and heraldic shields, braziers. Painterly 3D-illustrated slot style; solid forms, readable silhouettes.

## Palette
Night #0E1630 · Royal Blue #1C2E6B · Antique Gold #C9922E · Pale Gold #F4CF73 · Oxblood #7A1626 · Stone #6E6A64 · Parchment #F4E3B5

## Type
- Logo display: Cinzel Decorative Black (gold gradient, dark bevel stroke)
- Flat lockup and titles: Cinzel (tracked KINGDOM / gold rule / CROWNS)
- Interface: Barlow Semi Condensed
- Card letters: Cinzel Decorative; Q set in IM Fell English SC for a clear tail; wireframe letter labels in Marcellus
Not used: Inter Tight, Poppins, Cormorant, Manrope, any comic display face.

## Crown
Worked antique gold, royal blue enamel band with sapphires and rubies, oxblood velvet cap. Front reads as five points: 1, 3, 5 tall with rubies, 2 and 4 low with pearls, echoing the crown row of the grid (cell, gap, cell, gap, cell). Same crown is used in the logo, the Crown symbol, the app icon, on the king's head and in the intro crown drop.
This pass the crown is a studio still (generated and reviewed), not a Blender model: the deadline moved to 23:48 tonight, so no Cycles renders were run. A Blender model is a follow-up if Davit approves the design.

## King
One original adult king, mid-50s, short grey-brown beard, swept-back hair, royal blue embroidered robe, ermine collar, oxblood cloak. Seated on a carved gold throne. Reused in banner world, concept (left), wireframe base game (left, greyscale), storyboard and intro.

## Slot
5 × 3 grid plus a crown top row: cell, gap, cell, gap, cell (columns 1, 3, 5). Top cells hold wild, scatter or empty only.
Line pays exactly as given: Crown line 1000x (1/1000 chance to drop a Crown line), 7 500x, Sword 100x, Shield 75x, A 40x, K 30x, Q 25x, J 20x, 10 10x.
Wild and scatter: specials, no pays, no features invented. Win examples use a full 5-symbol line at Bet 1.00 (Shield line = 75.00, Crown line = 1,000.00) — how pays scale with bet and symbol count is not yet defined by Davit.

## Invented in this pass (design only, for review)
Sub-tag "A royal slot", concept name "The Crowned Hall", bet steps 0.20–10.00 and a 1,000.00 demo balance in wireframes, menu items. No free spins, multipliers or buy-bonus.

## Revision r4 (Davit: "make 7 more valuable")
- New 7: a jeweled royal artefact in the shape of a 7, the second-highest symbol (500x). Worked antique gold, the face paved with large faceted rubies in gold bezels, a sapphire pavé border, beaded rims and pearl corners.
- It's deliberately a different treatment from the royals (A, K, Q, J, 10 are cast gold with enamel inlay and a single small gem), so it reads as more precious at a glance. The bold, simple 7 silhouette is kept so it reads at cell size.
- Rejected:
  - opt1 (blue enamel plus filigree with gems): too close to the A/10 treatment.
  - opt2 (red enamel with a small crown finial): the finial competes with the Crown symbol, and the red enamel echoes K.

## r5 environments (three distinct grounds)
1. Kingdom exterior (banner, palace thumbnail): wide dusk valley, misty river, walled town left, cliff palace right, calm sunset band centred for the logo. Cool blue/violet with a warm horizon.
2. Throne room (game scene, slot beat): intimate stone nave, receding arches, warm torch sconces, oxblood and blue hangings at the edges, dark quiet centre behind the reels. Warm amber key, deep shadow.
3. Treasury (symbol sheet, concept board, storyboard, end card): top-down charcoal slate with an oxblood velvet fold and a royal-blue velvet fold in the corners, soft warm light pool in the centre. Neutral, tactile, low detail.
The difference comes from composition (panorama / architectural perspective / flat surface), depth and light, not from a recolour. The king and crown plates stay untouched and are composited with soft masks.
