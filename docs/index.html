Paytable · line pays

Only a full line of five pays. The prize is the multiplier times the total bet.

    The five lines

    Lines cross the 5×3 reels only. Left to right, row 0 is the top of that grid.

    • Top line — row 0 on reels 1 through 5.
    • Middle line — row 1 on reels 1 through 5.
    • Bottom line — row 2 on reels 1 through 5.
    • V — row 0, row 1, row 2, row 1, row 0. The point sits on the centre reel.
    • Inverted V — row 2, row 1, row 0, row 1, row 2.

    The crown row above reels 1, 3 and 5 is not a payline. Those cells show a wild, a scatter, or nothing.

    A wild on the crown row is wild for every row of that reel, on every line. Wild does not stand in for scatter. A line of five wilds pays nothing, because wild has no prize and there is no paying symbol for it to stand in for.

    The reel strips are weighted so a crown line — five crowns, or crowns completed by wilds — lands on about 1 spin in 1,000. Wins on different lines are added together. Three of a kind and four of a kind pay nothing.
